Install Scholarship Autofill

A side panel that fills the repetitive boxes on scholarship applications with answers you save once. You check every box before it fills, and it never submits anything. This copy works on a Chromebook, or in Chrome or Edge on any computer. Nothing else needs installing.

It can't install itself when you plug the drive in. Chromebooks (and Chrome) don't run anything automatically from a USB drive, and extensions from outside the Chrome Web Store have to be added by hand. It takes about two minutes, once per Chromebook.

On a Chromebook

  1. Copy the folder off the drive. In the Files app, open the USB drive, then Scholarship Autofill, then the version you want:
    • Mine (locked answers) comes with the owner's saved answers, locked with a passphrase.
    • For friends (blank) starts empty.
    Right-click the Scholarship Autofill folder inside it, choose Copy, open My files (or Downloads), and Paste.
    It has to live on the Chromebook. Loaded straight from the USB drive, it stops working when you unplug it.
  2. Open the extensions page. In Chrome, type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter.
  3. Turn on Developer mode with the switch at the top right of that page.
  4. Click Load unpacked, go to My files, click the Scholarship Autofill folder you pasted, and click Open.
  5. Pin it. Click the puzzle-piece button next to the address bar and pin Scholarship Autofill.
  6. Open a scholarship application and click the Scholarship Autofill button (or press Alt+Shift+F). The panel opens on the right.
    • Mine: type the passphrase once. Your answers are added to this Chromebook and the passphrase isn't asked again.
    • Blank: open My info and add your answers, or paste your transcript under Classes.
School Chromebooks: if there's no Developer mode switch or no Load unpacked button, the school has blocked extensions from outside the Web Store, and this can't be installed on that Chromebook. Use a personal one.

On Windows or Mac (Chrome or Edge)

The same steps: copy the folder to your computer (for example to Documents), open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and pick the folder. If the browser later asks to turn off developer-mode extensions, choose to keep it.

Using it

  • Point at boxes, Circle a group of them, or choose Whole page. Each box shows the exact text it will get. Nothing is typed until you press Fill, and Undo puts everything back.
  • It never clicks Submit, Next or Apply, and it blocks any submit the page tries while it fills. Read the form over and submit it yourself.
  • It leaves alone anything it isn't sure about, plus essays, signatures, "I certify" boxes, Social Security numbers and passwords.

Keep your answers safe

  • Your answers are saved in this browser only. Removing the extension, or resetting (powerwashing) the Chromebook, deletes them. Use My info → Back up or move your answers → Download a backup now and then, and keep that file private (it isn't locked).
  • To move to another computer: install it there, then choose Restore from a backup….
  • Anyone who uses your Chrome profile can open the panel and see your answers. Sign out, or use your own profile, on shared computers.

Updating

Copy the new Scholarship Autofill folder over the old one in My files, then press the reload arrow on its card in chrome://extensions. Your answers stay.
